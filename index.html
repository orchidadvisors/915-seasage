A fully reimagined coastal modern on a quiet east Boynton street, minutes to the beach and to downtown Delray's Atlantic Avenue.

Three bedrooms and two baths, a two-car garage, and a 10,000-square-foot lot on a quiet street east of I-95, a short drive to Oceanfront Park and the restaurants of Atlantic Avenue.
